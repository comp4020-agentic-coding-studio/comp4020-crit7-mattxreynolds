# 0014. The login page offers one-click demo students, named and random

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-09-28
- Decided by: Matt
- Source: #5

## Decision

Seeded **demo students** have one shared password, published in the README.
The login page lists the demo students by name, one click each, plus a
"Random demo student" button.

## Reason

Matt on Q6: "Yes. Include a button on the login page to login as a random
demo student". Q8: accepted the recommendation to list the named demo
students too, because showing accept/decline needs a tutor to log in as the
poster and then as the student who offered, which a random pick can't
guarantee.

## Consequences

Anyone can act as a demo student; the app says so (0015). Which demo students
exist and what they have posted stays in the seed-data fog on map #2.
